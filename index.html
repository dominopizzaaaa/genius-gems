About Us

A Place Where Children Flourish

At Genius Gems Childcare Centre, we believe every child carries a unique brilliance — and our role is to help it shine. Nestled in Changi, our centre offers a warm, structured, and joyful environment for children to grow holistically.

Our approach blends Singapore's ECDA framework with globally inspired early childhood methodologies, delivered by a passionate, highly qualified team who genuinely love what they do.

Our Difference

What Makes Us Shine

What sets Genius Gems apart — the heart, care, and dedication behind every day at our centre.

Hands-On Leadership

Privately Run Centre

Genius Gems is a proudly independent, privately run childcare centre — not a franchise, not investor-led, and not managed from a distant corporate office. Our director and principal are personally involved in the day-to-day running of the school, ensuring every child receives the consistent, attentive care that families deserve.

This hands-on approach means real accountability behind every decision, every classroom, and every moment of your child's day with us.

Close-Knit Community

Small Classes, Personal Attention

We intentionally keep our class sizes small so that every child is truly seen, known, and supported. Our educators take the time to understand each child's personality, interests, and pace of learning — tailoring their approach to bring out the very best in them.

Unlike large mega-centres, no child gets overlooked here. Children at Genius Gems enjoy genuine, warm relationships with their teachers, and the confidence that comes from being part of a tight-knit, caring community.

Joyful Learning

Learning Through Play, Every Day

At Genius Gems, every lesson is an adventure. Our educators weave play-based learning into structured activities — creating moments of discovery that children genuinely look forward to, day after day.

From letter puzzles on the classroom floor to creative group activities, we believe the best learning happens when children are happy, engaged, and feeling safe.

English & Mandarin

True Bilingual Immersion

Our 100% bilingual programme is far more than a daily Mandarin lesson. English and Mandarin Chinese are woven throughout the whole day: our dedicated Chinese educators speak, sing, and play with the children in Mandarin, while English literacy grows alongside through reading, writing, phonics, and expressive communication.

Young children absorb language through genuine daily use, not drills. Supported by enrichment such as Chinese Performing Arts and cultural appreciation, our gems grow comfortable moving between both languages — a strong foundation for primary school and a lifelong gift. Explore how this works in our A-LIVE curriculum.

Our Curriculum

Meet A-LIVE: Our Integrated, Hands-On Curriculum

A-LIVE is the name of our curriculum from Toddlers to K2. It connects stories, themes, literacy, numeracy, and social development so children learn through meaningful experiences, not isolated subjects.

A-LIVE, simply explained

One connected curriculum for the whole child

Each story or theme becomes a shared context for reading, early maths, discovery, creativity, movement, and interaction. Children understand why they are learning something because they can see, touch, discuss, and use it.

01

Connected

Learning areas come together through one story or theme.

02

Hands-on

Children explore through play, movement, talk, and practical activities.

03

Progressive

Experiences deepen with age and prepare children for primary school.

How A-LIVE grows with your child

What will my child experience at each stage?

The learning stays connected and hands-on throughout. As children grow, teaching becomes more structured and builds toward confident school readiness.

01

Toddlers to N1

Literature-based foundations

  • One storybook anchors two to three weeks of connected discovery.
  • Literacy, phonics, and early numeracy grow naturally from the story.
  • Play, movement, conversation, and hands-on activities make ideas meaningful.
02

N2 to K2

Themes plus specialist programmes

  • Integrated themes connect language, maths, inquiry, and everyday life.
  • Jolly Phonics provides structured literacy; Thalespin develops numeracy.
  • Reasoning, independence, and confidence deepen toward primary school.
A-LIVE in action

Learning children can see, touch, and experience

Thoughtful invitations turn each story or theme into real opportunities to investigate, create, practise, and connect with others.

Specialist learning from N2 to K2

Two trusted programmes within A-LIVE

A-LIVE is the overall curriculum. From N2 onward, these specialist programmes add a clear, structured progression for mathematics and English literacy.

Thalespin Numeracy

Locally developed numeracy programme

See it. Touch it. Understand it.

Thalespin bridges preschool and primary mathematics through concrete experiences that develop reasoning, logical thinking, and secure number understanding.

Concrete Pictorial Abstract
  • Big Book: each new idea begins in an engaging, meaningful context.
  • Hands-on practice: children reason with manipulatives before using an activity book.
  • Home partnership: a shared parent-child activity extends learning every five weeks.
Explore the official Thalespin website
Jolly Phonics

Multisensory synthetic phonics

From sounds to confident readers and writers

Jolly Phonics teaches through sight, sound, and movement, helping children progress from phonics to reading and writing, then onward through Jolly Grammar.

  1. 1
    Hear & actSound, song, action, and phonological awareness
  2. 2
    Blend & recogniseWord building, sight words, and playful practice
  3. 3
    Write & applyLetter formation, spelling patterns, and creative writing
