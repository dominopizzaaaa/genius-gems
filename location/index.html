Childcare Near Flora Drive, Loyang, Pasir Ris & Changi

Genius Gems Childcare Centre is at 26 Mariam Close, Singapore 508668 — one bilingual preschool serving families right across the East of Singapore.

Areas We Serve

We are a single centre, not a chain — one campus in a quiet close off Old Tampines Road, drawing families from across eastern Singapore. Whichever direction you are coming from, these pages set out the route, the nearest bus stops and what to expect on arrival.

Also Within Easy Reach

Beyond our four core neighbourhoods, we regularly welcome families from Tampines, Bedok, Simei, Changi Village and the Changi Business Park corridor. If you work at Changi Airport or in the Loyang industrial belt, our 7:00am opening and 7:00pm close are built for exactly that kind of roster.

One Address, One Team

Genius Gems is privately owned and run from a single site at 26 Mariam Close. That is deliberate: our director and principal are in the building every day, class sizes stay small, and decisions about your child are made by the people who actually teach them. We take children from 18 months to 6 years — playgroup, nursery, K1 and K2 — on the ECDA-aligned Blossom curriculum, with English and Mandarin used as living languages throughout the day.
