Childcare Near Pasir Ris — Genius Gems Preschool

Genius Gems is a bilingual childcare centre at 26 Mariam Close, Singapore 508668, roughly 3.5 km from Pasir Ris MRT (EW1) — close enough for a quick Pasir Ris drop-off, far enough to feel like the countryside.

A Quieter Alternative to the Town-Centre Centres

Pasir Ris is well served by preschools — but most of them sit inside HDB void decks, blocks and malls around the town centre, sharing the same morning crush and the same shortage of outdoor space. Families who want something calmer, greener and smaller often look just beyond the town boundary.

That is where we are. Genius Gems sits in a quiet residential close off Old Tampines Road, roughly 3.5 km from Pasir Ris MRT station — a short drive from Pasir Ris Drive, Elias Road and the estates around White Sands and Downtown East, but with a large outdoor playground and none of the town-centre congestion at 7:30am.

Getting Here from Pasir Ris

The nearest MRT station to the centre is Pasir Ris (EW1) on the East–West Line, approximately 3.5 km away. From the station it is a short hop by bus or car. By road, most Pasir Ris routes run via Loyang Avenue and Old Tampines Road, then into Mariam Way and Mariam Close — the centre is about 50 m along on the left.

Open live driving directions from Pasir Ris MRT, or see the full turn-by-turn directions.

Bus Services

Service 29 connects the Pasir Ris and Loyang side of the East to stops beside the centre at Aft Jln Mariam (97109) and Bef Jln Mariam (97101). Services 5 and 2 stop at Aft Toh Cl (97119), Opp Changi Women's Prison (97051) and Changi Women's Prison (97059), all within walking distance.

What You Get for the Extra Few Minutes

  • A large outdoor playground and real space to run — not a shared void-deck corner.
  • Small classes, deliberately capped so that no child is a number on a register.
  • Genuine bilingual immersion in English and Mandarin throughout the day.
  • Calm drop-off on a quiet close, rather than a queue on a main road.
  • Long hours — 7:00am–7:00pm weekdays, 7:00am–1:00pm Saturdays.

Our Programmes

We take children from 18 months to 6 years — playgroup, nursery and kindergarten (K1 and K2) — all on the Blossom curriculum, our ECDA-aligned, play-based framework. Excursions, phonics, sensory play and Chinese cultural appreciation all sit inside the weekly rhythm. Fees and subsidies are listed in full on the homepage.
