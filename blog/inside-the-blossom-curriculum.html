Inside the A-LIVE Curriculum: Story, Phonics and Singapore Math

16 July 2026 5 min read The Genius Gems Team

Every parent who tours Genius Gems eventually asks the same excellent question: "What will my child actually be doing all day?" Our answer is A-LIVE, a progressive curriculum that turns stories, themes, phonics, and mathematics into active experiences children can understand and enjoy.

Two Stages, One Clear Learning Journey

For Toddlers to N1, each lesson grows from a carefully chosen storybook. Early letter and number exposure is woven into the story, while hands-on activities remain the main focus. Children learn new ideas in a familiar context and build confidence through doing, talking, moving, and exploring.

From N2 to K2, learning becomes thematic. Each term connects language, numeracy, discovery, and everyday life through themes such as the body and health; family, friends, and school; community and the wider world; and nature, water, and inventions. The ideas deepen each year, giving children continuity as well as challenge.

Knowledge, Skills and Learning Dispositions

A-LIVE is designed to develop more than subject knowledge. Practical skills grow through projects and hands-on tasks, while positive learning dispositions develop through participation: curiosity, focus, confidence, and a willingness to keep trying. Clear lesson objectives, activities, closing reflections, and follow-up help teachers make that growth visible.

Thalespin Numeracy: Singapore Math Made Concrete

Our numeracy approach follows the concrete-pictorial-abstract progression. Children first handle real objects, then use pictures and visual models, and finally connect those experiences to numbers and symbols. The scope begins with essential pre-number ideas such as matching, sorting, comparing, ordering, and patterning. It then builds counting, number sense, shapes, and spatial thinking.

Jolly Phonics: From Sounds to Words

Synthetic phonics teaches children how spoken sounds connect to written language. Younger learners meet sounds through songs, actions, objects, games, and sensory letter practice. As they progress, children blend sounds into words, build sight-word strategies, explore spelling patterns such as alternative vowel spellings and Magic E, and apply their knowledge in spelling and creative writing.

How A-LIVE Builds School Readiness

By K2, children are doing much more than memorising answers. They can draw on concrete experiences to explain mathematical thinking, use phonics and spelling strategies independently, contribute to thematic discussions, and express ideas in writing. Just as importantly, small-group learning builds the focus, listening, and confidence they will carry into Primary One.

Learning Stays Active

Hands-on learning runs through every stage. Manipulatives, sensory materials, songs, movement, learning stations, and real-life connections help children make sense of each new concept. You can see examples of our active learning environment in the gallery.
